# General FAQ

## About the FAQ

How long does a company process take, how many steps are there, and where do employees find the answer? This FAQ collects common questions employees ask about company policy. Employees can search the FAQ, the company intranet, or ask a manager how long, how many and where for any company policy question.

## Costs

Most company services cost nothing for employees. Where a service costs euros, the amount in euros is listed on the intranet, and employees pay euros by card.
