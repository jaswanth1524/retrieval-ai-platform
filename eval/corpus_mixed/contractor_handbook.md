# Contractor handbook

## Scope

This handbook is for external contractors and agency staff, not employees. Employees follow the company policies instead.

## Invoicing

Contractors submit invoices and receipts through the supplier portal within sixty days. Contractor mileage is not reimbursed.

## Working location

Contractors may work remotely five days a week unless the statement of work says otherwise.

## Equipment

Contractors use their own laptops, which are not replaced by the company. Contractors may install tools their contract allows.
