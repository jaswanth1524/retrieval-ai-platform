# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely. Remote work is arranged with the line manager and recorded in the HR system.

## Schedule

Employees may work remotely up to two days a week. Team anchor days are set by each department, and everyone is in the office on anchor days.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a headset for remote work. Monitors and chairs for home offices are not reimbursed.
