# Remote Work Guidelines

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely. Remote work is agreed with the line manager and noted in the HR system.

## Schedule

Employees may work remotely up to three days a week. Each department sets one anchor day, when the whole team works from the office.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a headset and a monitor for remote work. A home office allowance of 300 euros a year covers a chair or a desk.
