# Company calendar

## Working days

The company works five days a week. Employees plan their days around the company calendar: office days, focus days and meeting days. Public holidays are days off, and bridge days between holidays are optional days. Every week has two meeting days and three focus days, and the company announces special days a week ahead. Days, weeks and months in the calendar follow the company year.

## Events

The company holds a summer party and a winter party each year. Employees get a reminder a week before each event, and the company books the venue months ahead.
