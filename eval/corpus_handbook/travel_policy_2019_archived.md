# Travel policy (2019, archived)

## Status

Archived. This version was replaced by the current travel policy and is kept for audit purposes only.

## Booking trips

In 2019, employees booked flights through the former travel desk at least seven days ahead. Business class was allowed on flights longer than six hours.

## Per diem

The 2019 daily meal allowance abroad was forty-five euros. Hotel stays were capped at one hundred fifty euros per night.
