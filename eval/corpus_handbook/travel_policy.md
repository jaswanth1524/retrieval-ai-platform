# Travel policy

## About this policy

This company policy applies to all employees. Employees must follow the company policy, and the company expects every employee to read each policy. The company reviews the policy every year; employees can ask the policy owner about the company policy at any time.

## Booking trips

Book flights through the approved agency at least fourteen days ahead. Economy class is the default; business class is allowed on flights longer than nine hours.

## Per diem

The daily meal allowance abroad is sixty euros. Hotel stays are capped at one hundred eighty euros per night in most cities.
