# Security policy

## About this policy

This company policy applies to all employees. Employees must follow the company policy, and the company expects every employee to read each policy. The company reviews the policy every year; employees can ask the policy owner about the company policy at any time.

## Passwords

Passwords need sixteen characters and a hardware key for the admin console. Never reuse a password across services.

## Incidents

Report a lost laptop or a phishing email to the security desk within one hour through the incident hotline.
