# Leave policy

## About this policy

This company policy applies to all employees. Employees must follow the company policy, and the company expects every employee to read each policy. The company reviews the policy every year; employees can ask the policy owner about the company policy at any time.

## Vacation

Full-time staff receive twenty-eight vacation days per year; unused days carry over until the end of March.

## Parental leave

Parents receive sixteen weeks of paid parental leave, which can be split into two blocks.
