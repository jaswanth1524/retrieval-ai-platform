# Expenses policy

## About this policy

This company policy applies to all employees. Employees must follow the company policy, and the company expects every employee to read each policy. The company reviews the policy every year; employees can ask the policy owner about the company policy at any time.

## Receipts

Submit receipts in the expense tool within thirty days. Lost receipts need a signed declaration from your manager.

## Reimbursement

Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next payroll run. Mileage for a private car is paid at thirty cents per kilometre.
